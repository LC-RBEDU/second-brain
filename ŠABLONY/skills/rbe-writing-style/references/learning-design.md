# Red Button EDU learning design

## Core architecture

Recurring RBE design pattern:

Samostudium / prework -> live experience -> practice or experiment -> share and reflection.

A common shorthand is I-E-S:

- Inspire: provoke, expose, frame, demonstrate.
- Experiment: try something in a real or simulated situation.
- Share: compare, reflect, learn from peers, and make the next move visible.

Use Kolb's experiential learning cycle as a design lens where appropriate: experience, reflection, conceptualization, experimentation.

## Practicality

Default toward learning by doing. Use real cases, role-play, simulations, decision moments, applied experiments, and participant-created outputs. Avoid designing a lecture with interaction sprinkled on top.

For AI/digital topics, integrate learning into real work wherever possible. AI should not become a separate theoretical module if the business task can carry the learning.

## Common formats

- 7-10 minute prework
- 60-90 minute webinar
- 3-hour online session
- half-day or full-day workshop
- 1+1 day format
- hackathon / ideathon
- 4-9 week sprint
- challenge cycles built around inspire / experiment / share

Online groups up to about 15 are a useful default for breakout-heavy work. Three hours is a strong online maximum/ideal for many practical sessions. Beginners often benefit from offline delivery.

## Measurement and diagnostics

Recurring methods include:

- K-S-A pre/post on a 5-point scale
- 360 feedback
- development/assessment center
- ADKAR
- stakeholder mapping
- Switch (rider / elephant / path)
- PrinciplesYou
- Hogan
- Gallup strengths when already available

Do not add diagnostics merely for sophistication. Connect measurement to a decision, behavior, or learning objective.

## Gamification and story

Use story and gamification when they reinforce the learning journey. Avoid decorative game mechanics that do not change participant behavior. Recurring mechanisms can include missions, keys, badges, role cards, branching choices, challenges, artifacts, or a light narrative frame.

## Learning objective style

Prefer observable capability language, for example:

- "Umím vysvětlit, proč se změna děje a proč teď."
- "Umím naplánovat změnu tak, aby lidé rozuměli procesu i své roli."
- "Umím pracovat s odporem bez zbytečného tlaku."

Avoid objectives that only describe exposure, such as "seznámí se" or "získá povědomí", unless that is truly the intended level.
